# theo-cloud knowledge

The repo-local knowledge an agent needs to reason about `theo-cloud` — the commercial
cloud-API (Go) and dashboard (React/TSX). Governance mandates, product surfaces, the
architecture of the seams this repo owns, roadmaps in flight, operator runbooks, the
meeting records that lock contracts, and release history.

Start here, follow the links. The binding contract for the codebase as a whole is the
repository `CLAUDE.md`; this bundle holds the documents that contract points at.

# Subdirectories

* [governance](governance/index.md) - Locked mandates and invariants a change must respect.
* [products](products/index.md) - Customer-facing product surfaces and their operations.
* [architecture](architecture/index.md) - How this repo's services and seams fit together.
* [roadmaps](roadmaps/index.md) - Work in flight, with its locked design decisions.
* [operations](operations/index.md) - Operator procedures, including the human-only gates.
* [meetings](meetings/index.md) - Review-board records that ratify a contract.
* [handoffs](handoffs/index.md) - Pointers into cross-repo programmes.
* [releases](releases/index.md) - What shipped, when, and against which review.
